## Phase 6 — Data-source integrations

Potential integrations should be added only where platform terms and customer authorization allow them.

- [ ] Google Business Profile for businesses the customer manages
- [ ] Shopify/review-platform exports
- [ ] App-store review sources
- [ ] Support-ticket/customer-feedback exports
- [ ] Scheduled private data imports

## Guiding principle

Do not add infrastructure simply because it is technically interesting. Each new phase should make the product easier to buy, easier to use, safer for customer data, or more useful for decision-making.
